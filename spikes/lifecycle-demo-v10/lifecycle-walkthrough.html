One application · six stages · nothing hidden

Rent assistance application

One small government form, from the sentence someone said out loud to a signed release to a change request that rebuilds it. Everything below is what actually happened — what the system allowed, what it refused, and what it lost.

What someone asked for

  1. B1A tenant behind on rent tells us about their household and what went wrong.
  2. B2Before it counts, they sign a declaration that what they said is true.
  3. B3They get a receipt they can show a landlord or a court.
  4. B4A caseworker works through the queue and decides.

What we set out to prove, and what happened

5 of 6 met
BAR 1 Met

Everything traces back to the original request

Every step from a sentence in the original request to a page, a question, and the field a person actually fills in is either a link the system recorded itself, or a reference sitting in the shipped files.
